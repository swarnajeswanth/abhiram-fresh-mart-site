3. Orders, pricing and availability

Products are sold by independent local stores. Prices, offers and availability are set by each store and may change at any time. The total shown when you place the order is what you pay, unless the order changes as described below.

Each order includes a pickup and delivery fee (based on the number of stores), and may include a handling fee and, during bad weather, a rain surcharge. All fees are shown before you place the order.

Each store must accept its part of your order. If a store declines or doesn’t respond in time, we’ll show you which items are affected and you can either continue with the stores that accepted — your items, fees and total are recalculated — or cancel the whole order. If no store accepts, the order is cancelled automatically.

We and our partner stores may refuse or cancel an order — for example if an item is out of stock, if there is a pricing or coupon error, or if delivery to your address isn’t possible. Orders over an unusually long distance may be held briefly for manual review before a delivery partner is assigned.

4. Payment, credits and coupons

All orders are paid in cash on delivery, to the delivery partner, when you receive your order. We do not collect card, UPI or bank details in the app.

Coupons and store offers are subject to the conditions shown when applied (for example a minimum order value or specific stores or items) and may be withdrawn or corrected if applied in error.

Referrals: a referral code can only be entered by a new customer, before their first order, and only once. The new customer’s referral credit is added once the customer who shared the code has had at least one order delivered. The customer who shared the code earns a referral credit when the new customer’s first order is delivered — the amount shown in the app for their first referred friend, and a smaller amount for each friend after that. Cancelled orders do not count.

Welcome and referral credits can only be used against the pickup and delivery fee and handling fee, have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and are deducted only when an order is delivered. We may change or end these offers, and may withhold or remove credits obtained through misuse, such as fake or duplicate accounts.

You will see a delivery code in the app. Share it with the delivery partner only when you have received your order — entering it confirms delivery.

You can cancel an order from the app until it is delivered. Please cancel as early as possible; repeated late cancellations may lead to restrictions on your account.

As payment is collected only on delivery, you are not charged for a cancelled order, and any credits applied to it are not used. If an item was missing, wrong or of poor quality, report it through "Report an issue" as soon as possible and we will work with the store to resolve it.
