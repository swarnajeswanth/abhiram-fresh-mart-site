Privacy Policy — Customer app

Last updated: August 19, 2026

AbhiRam FreshMart ("we", "us") operates the AbhiRam Fresh Mart app, a grocery ordering and delivery service. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

Account details: your mobile number (used to log in via a one-time SMS code), and optionally your name, email address, and a profile photo.

Location: your device's precise GPS location, and any delivery address you save (address line, area, pincode, and map coordinates). We use this to find nearby stores, calculate delivery routes, and show live order tracking.

Orders and payments: your cart contents, order history, delivery status, and coupons applied. Payment card or UPI details are handled by our payment provider directly — we do not store your card or bank details on our servers.

Photos: if you upload a profile picture, the image file itself.

Support requests: anything you submit through in-app issue/complaint reports.

Device and usage information: basic technical data such as app version and connection status, used to keep the app working reliably and to show you an offline/server-status screen when needed.

How we use this information

To create and secure your account, verify your phone number, and keep you logged in.

To let you browse stores, place and pay for orders, and see accurate delivery estimates.

To show your live order location and delivery route in real time.

To provide customer support and respond to issues you report.

To remember your preferences, such as saved addresses, language, and app theme.

Sharing with third parties

Delivery partners and the store fulfilling your order see what's needed to complete delivery: your name, phone number, delivery address, and order contents.

Service providers who process data on our behalf, such as our cloud image hosting provider (for profile photos), SMS provider (for OTP login), and payment processor (for checkout). They only receive what's needed to perform their service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Your choices

Location: you can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings; you'll need to enter a delivery address manually instead.

Profile photo: optional — you can remove or change it any time from Account.

Data export or questions: contact us at abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com.

Account deletion: you can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Account → Delete account in the app, or by emailing abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com from your registered email/number. This also removes your saved addresses and stops any further use of your data for the purposes above, subject to what we're legally required to retain (e.g. order/tax records).

Data retention

We keep your account and order data for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period after deletion where we're legally required to (for example, transaction records for tax purposes).

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (such as encrypted connections and access controls) to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is ever 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
