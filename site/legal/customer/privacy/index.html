Privacy Policy — Customer app

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Who we are

AbhiRam FreshMart ("we", "us") operates the AbhiRam Fresh Mart app, which lets you order groceries and other items from local stores in and around Vinjamur and have them delivered by our delivery partners. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

Account details: your mobile number (used to log in with a one-time SMS code), your name, and optionally a profile photo. If you add an email address on the Account screen, it is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

Location: your device’s precise location when you choose "Use current location" or pick a spot on the map, and the delivery addresses you save (address line, area, pincode and map coordinates). We use these to show nearby stores, calculate delivery routes and fees, and deliver your order.

Orders: your cart, the items and stores you order from, delivery status, order history, coupons and credits applied, cancellation reasons, and any ratings, reviews or delivery notes you add.

Payments: orders are paid in cash on delivery. We do not collect or store any card, UPI or bank details.

Credits and referrals: your credit balance, your referral code, and whether you joined through someone else’s referral code.

Photos: the image file, if you upload a profile photo.

Support requests: anything you submit through "Report an issue".

Device and technical information: your phone model, Android version, app version, IP address, and a random identifier created when the app is installed. We record these when you log in and when you open the app, to keep your account secure, to detect fraud and misuse (for example, several accounts being used from one phone), and to fix problems.

How we use this information

To create and secure your account, verify your phone number, and keep you logged in.

To show stores and products near you, place your orders, pass each store the items it needs to prepare, and assign a delivery partner.

To show your order’s live status, your delivery partner’s location, and an estimated arrival time while the order is on its way. Your own location is not tracked during delivery; the address you chose is used as the destination.

To tell you when a store can’t fulfil part of a multi-store order, so you can continue with the remaining items or cancel.

To apply welcome and referral credits, coupons and store offers.

To provide customer support, handle issue reports, and improve product and store quality using ratings.

To remember your preferences, such as saved addresses, language and app theme.

Sharing with others

The stores fulfilling your order and the delivery partner assigned to it see what they need to complete it: your name, phone number, delivery address and map location, any delivery note you add, and the items ordered.

Service providers who process data on our behalf, only as needed for their service: an SMS provider (to send login codes), a cloud image host (for profile photos), map and routing providers (pickup and delivery coordinates, to calculate routes and times), and our cloud hosting and database providers.

Your ratings may be shown as part of a product’s or store’s average rating.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users, stores or delivery partners.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Your rights and choices

Location: you can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings and type in or pick your delivery address instead.

Profile: you can change your name and change or remove your profile photo at any time from Account.

Access and correction: contact abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com to request a copy of your data or to correct it.

Account deletion: go to Account → Delete account in the app, or request it at https://abhiramfreshmart.fly.dev/legal/customer-data-deletion or by emailing abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com from your registered number. Your name, profile photo and saved addresses are erased immediately. For 30 days you can restore your account by logging in again; after that your phone number is released from the account. Records of orders you placed are kept for accounting and legal purposes.

If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you rights to access, correct and erase your personal data and to raise a grievance. You can exercise these rights using the contact details below.

Data retention

We keep your account information while your account is active. After you delete your account, your profile is erased as described above. Order and transaction records, and the device and security records linked to them, are kept for as long as needed for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention and legal purposes.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and access to it is restricted to the people who need it to run the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we’ll update the "last updated" date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
