Privacy Policy — Admin app

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Who we are and who this policy is for

AbhiRam FreshMart ("we", "us") operates FreshMart Admin, an internal management app used by our own staff to run the AbhiRam Fresh Mart grocery delivery platform — approving orders, and managing vendors, delivery riders, pricing, and platform security.

This app is not distributed to the general public and is not directed at customers. It is intended for authorized staff only. It is listed on Google Play because our staff install it on their own Android devices.

Because this app is where staff view and manage records belonging to customers, vendors, and riders, this policy covers two things: (1) the account data of the staff member using this app, and (2) how this app handles the personal data of customers, vendors, and riders that staff can see through it.

Information we collect — about the staff member using this app

Login credentials: your admin email address and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).

Security and audit logs: each login attempt (successful or failed), account lockouts, and administrative actions you take (approving/rejecting orders, resetting a vendor's login, adding/removing categories, changing pricing) are logged together with your IP address, device/browser user-agent string, and a timestamp. This exists so suspicious activity on the platform can be investigated.

Notification settings: whether you've granted this app permission to show local push notifications (e.g. Android notification permission) for new orders needing review.

Information we collect — about customers, vendors, and riders (via this app)

This app does not collect this data directly from customers, vendors, or riders — it displays and lets staff manage records that were already collected through the customer, vendor, and rider apps and stored on our shared backend.

Customer data visible here: name, phone number, delivery address, and order contents/history, shown only in the context of processing and approving an order.

Vendor data visible here: shop name, area/pincode, login email, status, and (if uploaded) a shop background photo. Admins can set or reset a vendor's login credentials.

Rider data visible here: name, phone number, vehicle type/number, live/last-known GPS location, and delivery status — used to assign orders and monitor active deliveries on a live map.

We do not use this data for advertising, and it is only accessible to authorized staff logged into this app.

How we use this information

To authenticate staff and keep admin accounts secure.

To let staff review and approve orders that need manual verification before a rider is assigned.

To manage vendor accounts (activate/suspend, reset login credentials, set catalog categories) and rider accounts (add/remove riders, monitor live delivery location and status).

To configure platform pricing (delivery fees, rider payouts, admin-review distance threshold).

To detect and investigate suspicious account activity (repeated failed logins, unexpected admin actions) via the security log.

To send this device a local notification when a new order needs review, if you've granted notification permission.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell any information accessible through this app, and we do not share it with advertisers.

The data shown in this app lives on our own backend infrastructure; no customer, vendor, or rider data is sent to a third party by this app itself beyond the cloud image host used when an admin uploads a vendor background photo.

Your choices

Notifications: you can deny or revoke this app's notification permission in your device settings; you'll still see review-needed orders in-app.

Staff account: you can change your admin email or password from Settings at any time.

Access to this app is granted and revoked by AbhiRam FreshMart directly — contact abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com if your staff access needs to be removed.

Customers, vendors, and riders wishing to access, correct, or delete their own data should use the relevant options in the app they use directly (customer, vendor, or rider app), or contact abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com.

Data retention

Security log entries (logins, admin actions) are retained for as long as needed for security and audit purposes.

Customer, vendor, and rider records shown in this app follow the retention terms described in the AbhiRam Fresh Mart platform's customer-facing privacy policy, since this app doesn't store a separate copy — it reads and writes the same backend records.

Children's privacy

This app is a staff tool and is not directed at or intended for use by children.

Security

Admin sessions use short-lived access tokens with refresh rotation. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Repeated failed logins can lock an account. Connections to our backend are encrypted in transit.

No method of transmission or storage is ever 100% secure, but we use industry-standard measures to protect the information accessible through this app.

Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

If you are located in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you the right to: access a summary of your personal data and how it's processed; request correction or erasure of inaccurate or no-longer-needed personal data; nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity; withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent); and file a complaint with us, and thereafter with the Data Protection Board of India, if you're unsatisfied with how a request was handled.

For staff members: contact abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com to exercise these rights over your own admin account data.

For customers, vendors, or riders: please use the corresponding option in the app you use directly, or contact abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com — requests concerning your data are handled there regardless of which of our apps you used.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, where required, notify affected staff in the app.
