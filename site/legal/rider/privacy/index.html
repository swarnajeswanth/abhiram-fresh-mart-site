Privacy Policy — Rider app

Last updated: September 18, 2026

AbhiRam FreshMart ("we", "us") operates the FreshMart Rider app, used by delivery partners to accept and fulfil delivery orders. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

Account details: your mobile number (used to log in via a one-time SMS code) and your name.

Vehicle details: the vehicle type and registration number you provide.

Location: your device's precise GPS location. This is collected continuously while you are toggled online, including while the app is minimized — while enabled (see "Background location" under Your choices below), the app runs a small background service with a persistent "You're online" notification specifically to keep this working reliably once you switch apps, rather than only while FreshMart Rider is the app on your screen. This lets us assign you nearby orders and show your live position to the customer and to us during an active delivery. Location reporting stops when you go offline or log out.

Delivery data: pickup/drop-off details, delivery status, delivery-confirmation OTPs you enter, trip history, and earnings.

Support requests: issue or complaint reports you submit, and cancellation reasons.

Push/local notification status: whether you've enabled order alerts, and your device's notification token where applicable.

Device and usage information: basic technical data such as app version and connectivity, used to keep the app working reliably.

How we use this information

To create and secure your rider account and verify your phone number.

To assign you nearby orders and calculate pickup/delivery routes.

To show your live location to the customer and to our operations team during an active delivery.

To confirm deliveries, calculate your earnings, and maintain your trip history.

To alert you the moment a new order is assigned, including while the app is backgrounded.

To review and resolve issues you report.

Sharing with third parties

Customers see your live location, name, and vehicle details only while you're actively delivering their order.

Service providers who process data on our behalf, such as our SMS provider (OTP login) and push-notification provider. They only receive what's needed to perform their service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Your choices

Location: only collected while you're toggled online; you can go offline or revoke location permission in your device settings at any time (this will prevent you from receiving new orders).

Background location: on by default so your position keeps reaching us while you're online and minimize the app — shown to you via a small ongoing notification. You can turn this off any time from Profile → Background location; location reporting may then pause once you switch apps until you reopen FreshMart Rider.

Notifications: order alerts can be turned on or off from Profile → Notification settings.

Account deletion: you can request deletion of your rider account and associated data from Profile → Request account deletion in the app, or by emailing abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com. We'll confirm and process this within 30 business days, subject to data we're legally required to retain (e.g. payout/tax records, and records needed to resolve any open trip disputes).

Data export or questions: contact us at abhiramfreshmartvmr@gmail.com.

Data retention

We keep your account, trip, and earnings data for as long as your rider account is active, and for a limited period after deletion where legally required (for example, payout and tax records).

Security

We use industry-standard measures (such as encrypted connections and access controls) to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is ever 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
